About AlyxCut

AlyxCut is a next-generation AI video clipping tool from AlyxFai (makers of AlyxFiles). Designed specifically for Windows creators and desktop browsers, AlyxCut transforms full-length videos into ready-to-publish vertical Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

🛡️

Privacy-First Architecture

Unlike cloud video services that upload your private recordings to remote servers, AlyxCut executes all speech recognition, content analysis, and video rendering 100% locally on your machine.

💎

Zero API Costs

No monthly subscriptions, no OpenAI tokens, and no hidden API bills. AlyxCut leverages open-source Whisper and WebAssembly to give you unlimited local generation.

🇮🇳

Native Hinglish Captions

Built-in phonetic transliteration automatically converts Hindi speech into natural Roman Hindi (Hinglish), formatted with high-contrast burned subtitle typography.

💻

Hardware Compatibility

Optimized for modern desktop browsers with WebGPU acceleration and automatic multi-threaded CPU/WASM fallback for broad compatibility across Windows devices.
